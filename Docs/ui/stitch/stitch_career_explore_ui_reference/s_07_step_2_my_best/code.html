My Best

Think of a moment you were genuinely at your best. What was happening? What made it that way?

“Leading the university garden project launch...”

I was at my best when we converted an unused quad courtyard into a permaculture plot over one rainy Saturday. 18 volunteers turned up soaking wet.

Key drivers: 1) Tangible immediate impact, 2) Shared physical purpose, 3) High trust with low micromanagement.

Notice how small or non-work contexts often reveal the purest truths about your natural energy.
